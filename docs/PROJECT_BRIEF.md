# PROJECT: ANIME-INSPIRED PREMIUM STREETWEAR E-COMMERCE WEBSITE

## 1. THE CREATIVE VISION

Build a completely custom, multi-page, anime-inspired streetwear e-commerce website specializing in upper-body clothing, including:

- Oversized T-shirts
- Graphic T-shirts
- Hoodies
- Oversized sweatshirts
- Custom-designed streetwear

IMPORTANT: This must NOT look like a standard Shopify store, a generic fashion template, or an AI-generated landing page.

The website should feel like an interactive anime cinematic universe combined with a premium Japanese streetwear fashion brand.

The customer should feel as if they are entering an anime world, not simply opening an online clothing store.

### Anime Art Direction

Use these anime productions as visual and creative references:

1. Akira (PRIMARY INSPIRATION): Neo-Tokyo atmosphere, cyberpunk architecture, cinematic red lighting, underground street culture and bold graphic compositions.
2. Jujutsu Kaisen: Dark cinematic atmosphere, dramatic visual effects, intense transitions and atmospheric lighting.
3. Cyberpunk: Edgerunners: Futuristic interfaces, neon accents, experimental typography and underground fashion culture.

Do not directly copy copyrighted anime characters, logos, scenes, or artwork. Develop an original visual identity inspired by their art direction.

## 2. DESIGN LANGUAGE

Develop a completely custom design system.

### Color palette

Avoid generic AI-generated purple-blue gradients and conventional e-commerce color combinations.

Explore a carefully balanced palette:

- Deep charcoal: #101010
- Near-black: #080808
- Blood red: #C51F2B
- Muted crimson: #741B24
- Warm off-white: #E8E1D5
- Concrete grey: #77736F

Use red as an intentional visual accent rather than applying it everywhere.

Allow occasional monochrome photography, subtle grain, manga-inspired graphic elements, and cinematic lighting.

### Typography

Use a distinctive combination of:

- Bebas Neue or Oswald for dramatic headlines.
- Space Grotesk for navigation and modern interface elements.
- Noto Sans JP for subtle Japanese-inspired typography.
- A carefully selected Japanese display font for occasional editorial details.

Use oversized typography, unusual text alignment, vertical Japanese-inspired text treatments, condensed lettering and carefully composed whitespace.

Do not randomly insert Japanese words. All Japanese text must have an intentional meaning.

## 3. WEBSITE STRUCTURE

Build a fully functional multi-page website with these separate routes:

1. Cinematic Preloader / Intro
2. Home
3. Collections
4. About Us
5. Contact Us

Also include individual product detail pages and a functional shopping cart.

All pages must share the same design language, navigation system, animation principles and visual identity.

## 4. CINEMATIC PRELOADER — MOST IMPORTANT

The first impression must be unforgettable.

Create a full-screen cinematic anime-inspired intro.

### Preloader sequence

PHASE 1 — CINEMATIC INTRO

- Display a full-screen video background.
- The video should have an intense anime-inspired cinematic atmosphere.
- Use dark visuals, dramatic lighting, action-oriented sequences and atmospheric sound design.
- The user will provide the actual anime-inspired video asset.
- Until the video is provided, create a clearly replaceable video asset placeholder.
- Add a minimal loading indicator and custom brand identity.
- Avoid conventional loading spinners.

PHASE 2 — CAMERA ZOOM

As the intro reaches its climax:

- Gradually zoom into the video.
- Increase visual intensity.
- Use smooth cinematic scaling.
- Coordinate the transition with the video timeline.

PHASE 3 — FOUR-PANEL CURTAIN TRANSITION

This is a critical animation.

At the end of the zoom:

- Divide the entire screen into FOUR equal vertical panels.
- Each panel should behave like an independent cinematic curtain.
- Animate the four panels away from the screen using carefully choreographed timing.
- Use slight staggered delays between panels.
- Maintain seamless visual continuity.
- The panels should reveal the actual homepage underneath.
- The transition must feel smooth, powerful and professionally directed.

The final result should feel like four cinematic curtains opening to reveal another world.

Avoid simple opacity fading or basic slide animations.

The animation should be implemented using GSAP and appropriate clipping, transform and timeline techniques.

The preloader must work correctly on desktop and mobile, with a reduced-motion fallback.
